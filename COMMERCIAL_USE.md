# Commercial Use Notes

This repository is prepared as an original commercial-facing Live2D handoff package for **Aurelia Scholar**, a warm-orange scholar companion character.

## Brand and IP cleanup

- Do not use third-party brand names, logos, slogans, UI marks, or company identifiers in the final model, PSD, texture atlas, stream overlay, merchandise, or promotional materials.
- The supplied reference image is treated as visual inspiration for palette, silhouette, and costume complexity only.
- Final commercial artwork should be redrawn or produced from an authorized high-resolution source, with all brand-like marks replaced by original symbols.
- The production symbol should use the original **Aurelia Starflower** mark instead of any third-party logo.

## Required commercial deliverables

- Fully layered PSD or equivalent source file with redraw/overpaint rights cleared.
- Cubism `.cmo3`, exported `.moc3`, texture atlases, physics, pose, expressions, and motions.
- Written confirmation that all fonts, symbols, props, and background assets are licensed for commercial use.
- QA report confirming the model can be used in streaming, paid content, social promotion, and merchandise contexts.

## Not legal advice

This checklist is a production safeguard and is not legal advice. For high-value commercial releases, review the final artwork and naming with qualified counsel.
